Internal Communication

Internal Communication & Collaboration Workshop

Align teams, communicate clearly, and work better together.

This practical, high-impact workshop is designed to improve communication, collaboration, and commercial awareness across internal teams.

Built around your organisation and the day-to-day reality of managing customers, the workshop focuses on helping teams collaborate more effectively, build stronger relationships, improve communication and identify opportunities for growth.

Purpose. To help internal teams become more confident, commercially aware, and effective in the way they manage customers - as well as improving cross-team working and communication with clarity, consistency, and impact.

What you'll learn

Participants will learn how to:

  • structure conversations and meetings more effectively
  • ask better questions to uncover needs and improve understanding
  • communicate updates, ideas, and recommendations more clearly
  • manage relationships more strategically across teams
  • listen more effectively and respond with greater understanding
  • handle challenge and pushback more constructively and confidently
  • recognise risks, blockers, and misunderstandings earlier
  • improve alignment around priorities, objectives, and next steps
  • contribute more effectively to business and client outcomes

Core workshop areas

  • motivation, personal impact, and team effectiveness
  • vocal tone, body language, and communication style
  • structuring conversations and meetings clearly
  • understanding customer and stakeholder needs and pressures
  • opening conversations effectively
  • questioning techniques for clarity, problem-solving and alignment
  • signposting and communicating clearly with confidence
  • positioning ideas and recommendations more effectively
  • listening skills and how listening affects outcomes
  • recognising risks, delays, and hidden blockers early
  • relationship building across departments and functions
  • proactive communication and value-led engagement
  • managing challenge, resistance, and differing viewpoints constructively
  • influencing outcomes and gaining commitment

How it's delivered

The session is delivered in a highly interactive, non-classroom format using a mix of trainer input, discussion, exercises, games and group learning. This ensures the content feels relevant, commercially focused, and immediately usable. A high-energy ‘what we learnt today’ session reinforces the key learnings and helps participants embed new skills.

Ahead of the session, dedicated one-to-one time is spent with each participant to understand individual challenges, confidence gaps, and performance blockers. This ensures the final workshop is highly focused, relevant, and built around the real issues affecting team and individual effectiveness.
